# Clarity Systems

01. PURPOSE OF THE WEBSITE

Create a personal brand website for Vita Shablii — Strategist · Founder · Advisor.

This is not a portfolio website, not a classic consultant website, not an online course marketplace and not a SaaS landing page.

The website should function as an intellectual ecosystem around Vita's way of thinking.

Its purpose is to:

establish Vita's positioning and authority;

communicate her point of view;

help visitors recognize their current business problem;

direct them toward the right solution;

present Vita's products as interconnected systems rather than disconnected offers;

convert visitors into product buyers, SYNTEZ ecosystem users or consulting clients.

The homepage should NOT explain everything.

Its journey is:

WHO IS VITA
→ HOW DOES SHE THINK
→ WHY SHOULD I TRUST HER
→ WHERE AM I / WHAT IS MY PROBLEM
→ WHICH SOLUTION FITS ME
→ PRODUCT / ECOSYSTEM / CONSULTING

Це відповідає логіці вже створеної структури homepage.



02. CORE BRAND IDEA

The website is built around one fundamental idea:

CLARITY BEFORE COMPLEXITY.

Vita does not add more marketing tools to an already complicated business.

She helps people understand:

what they are creating
→ for whom
→ why it matters
→ whether it can work
→ how it should enter the market
→ how all elements connect into one system.

Marketing is therefore not the starting point.

It is part of a larger system.

This idea must influence not only the copy but the entire visual architecture of the website.



03. POSITIONING

Do NOT position Vita simply as:

marketing expert with 13 years of experience.

Experience is proof, not positioning.

The broader territory is:

Strategy × Marketing × Product Thinking × AI

Vita works at the intersection of these disciplines.

Her role is to transform:

ambiguity → clarity

ideas → viable concepts

separate actions → structure

marketing chaos → systems

intuition → informed decisions

complexity → understandable direction

Her experience should reinforce that proposition rather than become the proposition itself.

05. VISUAL WORLD

Core aesthetic

EDITORIAL × ORGANIC × INTELLECTUAL × TACTILE × SCIENTIFIC

Imagine the intersection of:

an independent editorial magazine;

a contemporary strategy studio;

an art book;

a scientific diagram;

natural materials;

quiet European fashion/editorial photography.

The website should feel art-directed rather than assembled from UI components.



06. COLOR WORLD

Use a restrained natural palette inspired by the supplied references.

Primary background:

warm ivory / natural paper

Secondary tones:

stone
sand
warm taupe
smoky grey
sage
muted olive

Dark typography:

charcoal rather than pure black.

Optional accent:

a muted mineral / moss / warm metallic tone.

Avoid bright digital colors.

Important

Do NOT create a rigid alternating pattern:

white section → green section → white section → beige section.

The site should feel like one continuous visual environment.

Color transitions can happen through photography, texture and subtle tonal shifts.



07. TEXTURE

Texture is important.

The website should not look digitally sterile.

Use extremely subtle:

paper grain;

film grain;

blurred light;

translucent surfaces;

water-like distortion;

shadow;

natural material texture.

But:

texture must remain atmospheric, not decorative noise.

Never sacrifice readability.



08. TYPOGRAPHY

Typography should carry a significant part of the art direction.

Use a combination of:

Editorial serif

For:

large statements;

emotional emphasis;

selected headlines;

brand moments.

Contemporary neutral sans-serif

For:

navigation;

body copy;

labels;

numbers;

product information;

UI.

Avoid excessive font variation.

Use typography through:

scale
spacing
alignment
contrast
italic treatment

rather than through many fonts.

Large headlines should sometimes behave like editorial compositions rather than normal web headings.



09. GRAPHIC LANGUAGE

This is one of the most important elements.

Create a recurring graphic system based on:

lines
orbits
intersections
nodes
circles
spirals
connected systems

The inspiration is somewhere between:

scientific drawing;

systems diagram;

geometry;

natural growth patterns;

orbital movement.

The line is NOT decoration.

It represents Vita's way of thinking.

The website begins with ambiguity.

As the visitor moves through it, the line gradually becomes a system.



10. THE CENTRAL VISUAL METAPHOR

Business is not a collection of isolated marketing activities.

It behaves more like a connected structure.

Different elements affect each other:

IDEA

The graphic system should communicate interdependence.

This connects especially well with the way Vita describes businesses as interconnected structures rather than isolated functions.



11. MOTION PRINCIPLE

Motion should be subtle and meaningful.

No flashy animation.

Use:

slow line drawing;

gentle image reveals;

subtle parallax;

text appearing through opacity/position;

diagram nodes activating;

extremely subtle texture movement.

Scrolling should feel like discovering a system.

Never animate everything.

Static moments are important.



12. GRID & COMPOSITION

Do NOT build every section using:

headline
paragraph
3 cards
button.

Break the grid intentionally.

Use:

large whitespace;

asymmetric compositions;

unexpected typography placement;

large images cropped editorially;

small labels contrasted with oversized statements;

occasional centered compositions;

diagrams integrated into layouts.

However, the underlying grid must remain disciplined.

The visual result should feel:

free on the surface, extremely structured underneath.



13. CTA SYSTEM

Do not scatter buttons everywhere.

SYNTEZ → Ecosystem

Use maximum:

one primary CTA per semantic area.

Secondary text links are allowed where appropriate.

Buttons should feel editorial and restrained.

Avoid oversized pill buttons.

33. MOBILE BEHAVIOUR

Mobile must NOT simply be the desktop site stacked vertically.

Recompose intentionally.

On mobile:

use large typography;

allow statements to occupy almost the entire viewport;

reduce decorative elements;

retain the line/system motif;

keep product discovery visually connected;

maintain generous whitespace;

use photography edge-to-edge where useful.

Avoid turning the three solutions into generic stacked cards.

They should still feel like parts of one system.



34. RESPONSIVE PRINCIPLE

Design for:

1440px desktop;

1024px tablet;

390px mobile.

Use fluid typography with clamp() where appropriate.

Set sensible maximum content widths.

Do not allow long Ukrainian text lines to become too wide.

Target approximately 45–70 characters per line for reading copy.



35. INTERACTION RULES

Links:

subtle underline / arrow movement.

Buttons:

quiet transitions.

Images:

minimal scale/parallax where appropriate.

Diagrams:

line-drawing or node activation.

Navigation:

smooth but fast.

Respect:

prefers-reduced-motion.

Performance is more important than decorative animation.



36. UI COMPONENT RULE

Reuse structural logic without making the site look componentized.

Buttons should share a system.

Labels should share a system.

Typography should share a system.

But individual sections should not all share the same visual template.

This distinction is crucial.

Systematic ≠ repetitive.

46. FIRST LOVABLE BUILD INSTRUCTION

Оце вже можна вставляти після master context як перший production prompt:

Build the homepage first.

Do not build secondary pages yet.

Use the full VITA SHABLII Master Brief as the source of truth for strategy, visual direction, content hierarchy and UX.

Before coding, internally map the homepage into its semantic sections and establish one coherent design system for typography, spacing, grid, buttons, lines and image treatment.

Then implement the homepage as one continuous editorial experience rather than a collection of independent landing-page sections.

Pay particular attention to the transition of the organic line throughout the page: it begins as an abstract visual element in the Hero, becomes a strategic diagram around “Marketing is not the starting point”, connects the solution ecosystem, and resolves into the IDEA → CLARITY → STRUCTURE → SYSTEM → GROWTH method.

The three offers — Marketing Set, SYNTEZ and Consulting — must appear within one connected visual composition. Do not use three identical cards.

SYNTEZ should receive slightly more visual gravity without making the other solutions appear secondary or irrelevant.

Use temporary editorial image placeholders where final Vita photography is not yet available. Clearly structure image components so photography can easily be replaced later.

Prioritize composition, typography, whitespace and information hierarchy over decorative UI.

Create desktop and mobile layouts intentionally rather than simply stacking desktop sections.

Implement subtle motion only where it reinforces meaning.

Do not invent testimonials, client results, product features, prices, awards, credentials or factual claims that are not provided in the brief.

The first output should feel like a highly art-directed personal brand website, not a finished e-commerce funnel.

North star: clarity before complexity.

All pages must be on English language
